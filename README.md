# BONSAI3D

Standalone NL/EN website: https://rubenschmeitz.github.io/bonsai3d/

The site is served directly from the root of the main branch using GitHub Pages.
Use `python3 -m http.server 8851` for local review. No build or dependencies are required.

The curated catalogue, finish choices, product galleries and product/finish links work in the browser.
The homepage pairs a short introduction with a three-image gallery (buttons and
arrow keys; no automatic rotation), three photographic category entrances and
three additional category links. Primary navigation has Collection, The atelier
and Custom; the brand logo opens Home. Maker stories and finish comparisons are
combined on `atelier.html`; old `afwerkingen.html` links preserve query parameters
and redirect to its finishes section. The estimator stays on `maatwerk.html`.
The examples page and its old composer alias remain removed. Catalogue records,
photographs and their provenance labels are preserved.

Private workshop data, manufacturing source paths, research archives and the other brand
are excluded. Font licences are included beside the fonts.

The October 2026 collection now contains 40 objects across six categories: ten tables/jitas, nineteen miniatures, two pots, two accent-plant designs, two stone bases and five presentation details.
New miniatures are generated concepts, have not been printed and have no production
geometry. Size, finish, details and manufacturability are agreed through an enquiry.
Their current photographic images depict matte-black resin miniatures with
modest light and ordinary surface irregularities. Six new detailed proposals add
bark, feather, fur, fabric and tool relief, all black only, beside living bonsais.
They appear in the miniature category.
Their indicative guide prices are EUR25–40, excluding living trees and styling.
Legacy miniature preferences remain preserved. These are generated examples, not
photographs of existing printed stock. Earlier v1/v2 images and matching black
transparent derivatives are preserved separately. Earlier v3 workbench views
remain in the galleries beside the new exhibition images.
The lamella pot, bark pot and openwork plateau remain outside the visible collection.

Twenty-one owner photographs have separate conventional corrections in
`assets/prints/natural/`: light tonal corrections, cropping, colour and sharpness.
Original public photographs are unchanged; corrected product views link to them.
Previous studio edits and generated finish images are preserved separately. The
front-facing product views use generated exhibition settings; natural photographs remain in the galleries. Generated previews have
explicit labels and are not evidence of a newly printed product or physical result.

Two earlier images under `assets/examples/` illustrate a flowering bonsai on the owner's
wood-look table and a cascade on the matte-black open root stand. They were created
with built-in Imagegen using existing bonsai/product photographs as references.
They are labelled generated inspiration, with direct links to the existing objects.
Trees and other styling are examples; these pictures do not document a new physical
print or a supplied arrangement. The original images and existing catalogue objects are preserved; the jita
now uses the owner's actual photograph instead of the former generated organic view. No scene selector, scene-sharing or composer enquiry remains.

The active renderer is `assets/collection.js`, with editorial sections in
`assets/collection-editorial.js` and `assets/collection.css`. The five content pages

## Atelier identity and pricing revision — 12 October 2026

The native SVG mark connects an asymmetrical branching tree with print layers.
Close generated photographic compositions show each table with a bonsai, each jita
with an accent planting,
each pot with a matching tree and each miniature beside a living example bonsai.
52 new images include three original signature designs with root, branch-lattice
and contour details and six exhibition accessories. All originals remain available.

Raw black/grey is distinct from hand-worked wood look. Grey is an enquiry colour
preference and explicitly has no generated preview yet. Catalogue guide prices
are EUR10–100 per object. Only pots have a stock indication, based on the owner's
confirmation; exact size, colour and quantities need confirmation. Other items
are on enquiry, with unprinted designs identified as proposals.

`maatwerk.html` estimates a custom piece by object type, dimensions, detail, new
design work, finish and quantity. Its assumptions are provisional retail
estimates, not measured printing costs. Larger/complex configurations and wood
finish on a pot require discussion instead of an automatic price. No automatic
quote, order, payment or submission occurs. Drafts include chosen prices and
calculator details and remain copyable/downloadable locally. Trees, plants,
stones, styling and delivery are excluded.
